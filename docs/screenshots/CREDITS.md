# Credits for the artwork in the VodMax screenshots

The screenshots use demo data: invented channels and guide, the Blender Studio open movies, and a few invented titles.
Every poster, backdrop, cast tile and episode still was cropped and resized from the source in its row; nothing else was changed.

- **Blender Studio open movies, CC BY, studio.blender.org.** Posters and stills of Sintel, Big Buck Bunny, Tears of Steel,
  Elephants Dream, Cosmos Laundromat, Spring, Sprite Fright, Coffee Run, Charge and Wing It! are © Blender Foundation /
  Blender Studio under Creative Commons Attribution (the version is in each row).
- **No studio logos.** The title logos are wordmarks rendered for the demo in free fonts (SIL Open Font License), not the
  films' logos, which are trademarks outside the Creative Commons licence.
- **Agent 327** is a Blender Studio film under CC BY-ND, so none of its artwork is used: its poster, backdrop and cast tiles
  are unrelated public-domain or CC0 photos. "The Northern Light", "Blue Harbour", "Night Train" and "Coastline" are invented
  titles illustrated only with public-domain or CC0 images.
- Channel logos and the live test pictures are generated by the demo.
